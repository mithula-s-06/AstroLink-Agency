About AstroLink Agency

AstroLink Agency is a comprehensive space mission management platform designed to streamline the control and monitoring of satellite missions, ground operations, and crew activities across multiple organizations including ISRO, NASA, and ESA.

Key Features:

  • Mission Management: Plan, track, and manage complex space missions with budget tracking and status monitoring
  • Satellite Operations: Monitor satellite health, battery status, operational life, and orbital information
  • Ground Station Control: Manage ground stations worldwide with uptime tracking and antenna specifications
  • Anomaly Detection: Track and respond to system anomalies with severity levels and automated alerts
  • Crew Logging: Maintain detailed crew activity logs with timestamps, roles, and task documentation
  • Telemetry Monitoring: Real-time satellite telemetry including altitude, velocity, temperature, and signal strength
